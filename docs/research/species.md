# Phase F life roster and movement systems

This is the deduplicated roster from [the 13 site briefs](sites.md). Each site link there leads to expedition, OBIS or published records. **Size** is an approximate model target, not the maximum measured body size. **Depth** is the proposed _in-game placement band_ supported by the cited local or nearby evidence, not the species' global range. `†` means the source supports only the listed higher taxon; use a generic Journal label, never a fabricated species epithet. `Regional` means comparable-depth nearby habitat rather than a sighting on the named wreck. `Unconfirmed` means no glow animation without a species-specific source. Colour is a white-LED palette target.

Site codes: [LC](sites.md) Lost City; [AX](sites.md) ASHES; [BE](sites.md) Beebe; [CD](sites.md) Challenger; [KA](sites.md) Kamaʻehuakanaloa; [HU](sites.md) Hunga; [MC](sites.md) Monterey; [HC](sites.md) Hudson; [BP](sites.md) Blake; [BH](sites.md) Blue Hole; [TI](sites.md) Titanic; [BI](sites.md) Bismarck; [EN](sites.md) Endurance.

## 1. Schooling and darting fish

One steering system: separation/alignment/cohesion, occasional feeding turns, and a low-cost individual dart. Keep schools near the lighted shallow rim or upper slope; there is no evidence for tropical reef schools at an 850 m caldera bottom. ## 4. Midwater drifters and chains

One buoyancy rig with advection by current and small periodic pulses; colony chains can share the same motion engine with a different skeleton. _Nanomia_ is only about 30 cm at maximum in MBARI's profile, and its local observation depth is surface–700 m. [MBARI _Nanomia_](https://www.mbari.org/animal/common-siphonophore/), [MBARI balloon worm](https://www.mbari.org/animal/balloon-worm/).

## 8. Cephalopods, microbial films and scan-only microfauna

The octopus needs one independent arm-and-mantle rig. Films and foraminifera are shader/scanning content, not steerable agents; grouping them here leaves the same eight production buckets. Keep microscopic organisms out of visible swimming populations. [NOAA Hudson octopus](https://sanctuaries.noaa.gov/hudson-canyon/), [Challenger foram record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0967063709002192), [Titanic rusticle bacterium](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20061494/).

**Implementation caution.** Only a subset of entries above are species-level organisms suitable for an individually named creature model; many others are microscopic, fixed, higher-rank, or regional proxies. A 20–30-model launch should prioritize depth-correct silhouettes and reuse the eight rigs across sites. In particular, Hunga floor and the 3,000–3,600 m canyon floors need new depth-matched survey evidence before adding named fish; Bismarck and Endurance need wreck-specific identifications before claiming their regional proxies as residents. [Hunga survey](https://niwa.co.nz/hazards/mapping-tongan-eruption), [NOAA Hudson research gap](https://www.deepseacoraldata.noaa.gov/regions/northeast), [Endurance OBIS limitations](../../data/landmarks/endurance/species.json).
