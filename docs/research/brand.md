# Phase F brand research

**Screened 2026-10-01, approximately 13:33–13:46 UTC.** Brief for F3-BRAND-UI; follows [the Phase F plan](../../plan/PHASE-F-PLAN.md). Recommendations are design judgments; sourced observations are separated below.

**Recommendation: Bathyline**, pronounced **BATH-ee-line**, with **“Explore the real deep.”** It connects the game's strongest differentiator—real underwater terrain—to a compact contour-map logo. No exact game or app match surfaced in this screen. It is **crowded**, rather than cleared: an unrelated furniture seller uses the exact word. **Depth Lantern** and **Seafloor Signal** are the alternatives; both also have related-word conflicts that deserve review.

The positioning sentence for all three: **A cinematic ocean exploration game. Real terrain, simple controls, discoveries worth finding.** Use “Play in your browser” and “Touch, keyboard or controller” as supporting copy. Present the world as an expedition, with life, scanning and the Journal carrying the identity.

## Scope and interpretation of the conflict check

- **Conflict:** exact game/app/developer use, or a particularly close name in relevant software. Eliminate from this implementation shortlist.
- **Crowded:** unrelated commercial use or related-word game/app use; the full proposed title was not found as an exact game/app title. Keep only with the qualifications stated.
- **Clear:** no relevant use surfaced in the bounded indexed search. This would mean a provisional search result, never a legal conclusion. None of the final fifteen earns an unqualified clear rating after checking similar names.

For each name, searched its quoted spelling with `game app trademark`, with `trademark`, and with `(site:store.steampowered.com OR site:itch.io OR site:apps.apple.com OR site:play.google.com)`. The two new compounds also received quoted-name-only searches and joined/spaced variants (`DepthLantern`, `SeafloorSignal`, `Sea Floor Signal`); Bathyline also received `Bathy Line`. Results sometimes broadened the requested phrase, so a partial-word result is explicitly identified as such. Store coverage is **web-index coverage**, not a complete native store catalog search. Trademark coverage is a quick web screen; cited aggregators are leads, with their reported statuses, rather than independently verified current register results. No exact trademark record surfaced for Bathyline, Depth Lantern or Seafloor Signal.

Positive findings below have direct source links. “Not found” describes this research session, not proof of absence. Before adopting the name publicly, the orchestrator should choose a full title and check that exact title and close variants in the relevant official trademark registers; [USPTO's search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search) is the starting point for a US check. No registration, purchase or reservation was performed.

## Top three: concrete identity concepts

### 1. Bathyline — recommended

**Tagline:** “Explore the real deep.”

**Why:** A concise science-derived word with enough room for wrecks, life, surveying and future real sites. The contour metaphor can organize the whole identity: logo, title animation, map selection and scanning completion. Cost: introduce its pronunciation once in the internal brief, and review unrelated commercial name use.

**Logo:** An original rounded circular viewport containing two stepped depth-contour curves; the outer curve and central vertical notch hint at a **B** without requiring a readable letter. A small gap in the contour suggests a navigable path. Use three simple SVG paths, `viewBox="0 0 24 24"`, 2-unit strokes and round caps. At 16 px, remove the inner curve and retain the viewport + single contour. Cream on navy for the wordmark; cyan only for the contour. No glow is needed for recognition. Wordmark: `Bathyline`, normal title case, moderately spaced, kept separate from the icon.

### 2. Depth Lantern — emotional alternative

**Tagline:** “Carry light into the deep.”

**Why:** Easy to say, inviting and tied to the cinematic headlight experience. It welcomes casual phone players. Cost: less explicit geography, and the existing Lantern deep-sea game weakens distinctiveness; always use the full two-word title.

**Logo:** An original pressure-sphere circle with a solid downward triangular beam terminating on one curved seafloor line. At 16 px, retain only the sphere and beam; no literal lantern handle or miniature sub details. Three flat shapes in one colour, 24-unit grid, with at least 2-unit gaps. The beam can become a gentle title-screen reveal, with a static version for reduced motion.

### 3. Seafloor Signal — expedition alternative

**Tagline:** “Every dive reveals more.”

**Why:** Its nouns directly communicate mapping and discovery, and fit research points, scanning and the Journal. Cost: longer title and related Seafloor Games/Abyss Signal identities; retain both words on title/meta/manifest surfaces.

**Logo:** An original low contour line under a central filled dot, with two upward sonar arcs. At 16 px, keep one arc and the contour. Four paths/shapes at full size, one colour, no fine grid. A broad horizontal lockup suits the desktop menu; stack the two words beside the symbol on narrow screens.

All three are **new logo concepts to author**, not sourced downloadable assets. Review silhouette and recognizability in monochrome at 16, 32 and 180 px before choosing. Do not import another game's logo, screenshot, interface art or audio from the reference links.

### Palette with calculated contrast

Proposed UI colours are authored choices, inspired by a dark expedition viewport. Keep the **interface** palette stable while the underwater scene changes with depth. Essential text and controls sit on opaque `#102B3A` plates; use glass translucency around those plates so readability does not depend on the current rendered scene.

### HUD, touch and menus: implementation brief

- **Visual grammar:** Short instrument rails, flat SVG icons, 1 px decorative hairlines, 8–12 px radii and generous spacing. Outer glass may use `rgba(16,43,58,0.94)` with the opaque text/control plates above. Blur is optional on desktop; use flat plates on phones. Do not dim the whole view for the ordinary HUD.
- **Default live HUD:** One compact depth/heading/above-seafloor rail; a small hull/battery area only when those systems are relevant; one current-objective line; `Scan`, `Photo`, `Journal`, `Pause` actions. Preserve the center for the world and the scan reticle. Put diagnostic/full telemetry behind an expandable instrument panel.
- **Copy:** Use `Depth`, `Heading`, `Above seafloor`, `Battery`, `Hull`, `Scan`, `Photo`, `Return`, `Dive`, `Daily Dive` and `Free Dive`. Show units beside values: m, °, %, m/s. Use `Scanning…`, `Lifeform recorded`, `Site recorded` and `Signal found`; instrument styling should not turn ordinary actions into acronyms.
- **Warnings:** Amber caution and coral critical states must include explicit text and a distinct icon. Use the existing game's threshold logic rather than inventing pressure/depth values in the brand layer. A successful scan gets a short mint outline and one small confirmation card.
- **Phone layout:** Start with 48 × 48 CSS px targets and at least 8 px separation as a product design target. Left thumb moves; right thumb owns scanning and primary actions. Keep ascend/descend reachable as already specified by touch implementation. Reserve safe-area insets; portrait uses short top rails and bottom thumb zones, landscape keeps the same action ordering. Labels remain readable at 320 CSS px width; never require hover.
- **Menus:** Live title scene with one primary `Dive` action; nearby `Daily Dive`, `Free Dive`, `Journal` and `Settings`. Site selection shows one large scene card with factual site information drawn from existing sourced content. One gentle contour/beam animation is enough. Pause provides an explicit resume action; confirmation sheets and discovery cards do not obscure touch controls during active play.
- **Motion and sound:** Scan confirmation uses a brief opacity/scale transition, not a full-screen flash. Respect reduced motion; retain static focus/selection states. UI sound reinforces an action but never carries its only confirmation.

## Decisions and acceptance checks for the orchestrator

1. **Choose the title:** recommend Bathyline after reviewing the unrelated furniture use; alternatives are Depth Lantern and Seafloor Signal with their documented related-name risks. Do not use Hadal/Hadal Atlas, Benthos, Abyssal or Trenchlight merely because a domain lookup is promising.
2. **Resolve the domain separately:** rerun the chosen `.com`/`.game` lookup and a registrar availability check at decision time. No `.io` availability claim is established here.
3. **Choose font payload:** approve the two-family DM Sans/Source Serif 4 direction, with IBM Plex Mono optional; synchronize the typography section of the art bible.
4. **Review the authored mark/UI:** monochrome favicon at 16 px; wordmark at narrow-phone width; 320 px portrait and phone landscape HUD; safe areas and 48 px touch targets; keyboard focus; reduced motion; composed contrast over shallow/vent/deep scenes. Verify all public title strings, HTML metadata and PWA names agree with the chosen full title when implementation lands.
