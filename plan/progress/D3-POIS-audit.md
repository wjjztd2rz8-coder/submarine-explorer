# D3 POI audit

Scope: every original mission objective and every POI without an objective in the 13 shipped sites. "Unmarked" means no placed prop within the scan radius; named terrain features are retained only where the bathymetry gives a visible basin, wall, mound, channel, rift or drop-off and the Journal teaches a site-specific fact. Existing props were checked against POI coordinates. Source notes and links live in each site's `sources.md`; the [Great Blue Hole source notes](../../data/landmarks/great-blue-hole/sources.md) include the new west-slope profile.

Verdicts: **Keep 38, Promote 7, Remove 27, Add 1**. The new western drop-off is the one addition. All 13 missions now have at least two primary objectives.

## Owner decision

The Great Blue Hole itself cannot be a useful underwater scan in the current GMRT tile: its real position is a flat, shallow cell. The mission now scans the two visible atoll slopes and keeps the hole's history in the Journal. A dedicated high-resolution bathymetry tile would allow a future scan of the sinkhole itself.
