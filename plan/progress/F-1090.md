## Fixed

**Chase camera crossed submerged ridges when the final eye was clear.**
`CameraRig.clampToTerrain` tested intermediate terrain against sea level rather
than the chase arm's height. A fully submerged ridge therefore passed the test
even when it obstructed the submarine-to-eye line. The rig now retracts at the
first sampled obstruction against the actual arm and refines that boundary.
The existing surface ceiling, first-person/photo behavior and recovery from
an invalid below-floor anchor remain covered by their original assertions.

Reproduction: submarine `(0, -120, 0)` above a `-200 m` floor, a triangular
ridge peaking at `-85 m` at `z=45`, and the default chase eye at `z=90`.
Before the fix the new test failed with `expected 90 to be less than 45`.
Afterward the eye retracts before the ridge and the whole sampled arm retains
the configured 6 m clearance, in desktop and portrait.

Changes: `src/sub/CameraRig.ts`, its architecture documentation, and
`tests/unit/bughunt1090.test.ts`. The latter also covers all 13 production
openings on Low/Medium, free-dive and mission hulls, desktop 1280×800 and
portrait 390×844, 3,600 real physics ticks, visible hull vertices, submarine
prop contacts, eye clearance and intermediate chase-arm terrain/prop clearance.
Monterey, Beebe and Titanic additionally undergo full 360° boat turns at 2°
intervals and a downward free-look drag. Titanic's minimum swept-arm clearance
is 6.000011 m; Monterey's is 23.622 m and Beebe's is 15.045 m.

**Mission selection readiness raced document replacement in F-960.** The
external failure occurs at `f-bughunt-960.spec.ts:96`, immediately after tapping
the home mission button. The button assigns `window.location.href`; touch
completion can precede navigation commit. `clockFramesUntil` could consequently
evaluate the home game's still-true ready flag, or lose its execution context
when the destination committed. Selection now arms `page.waitForURL` for the
exact selected mission before the tap and awaits `DOMContentLoaded` before
pumping readiness frames. The shared helper and every briefing, scan, Journal,
debrief, save and readiness assertion are unchanged. No catch-and-retry,
timeout increase, arbitrary delay or test skip was added.
