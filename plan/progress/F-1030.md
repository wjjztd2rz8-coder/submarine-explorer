# F-1030 — Blue Hole bowl ledges and horizon

## Changes

- Replaced height quantisation with broad limestone treads and steep risers in
  `terrainFeatures.ts`, including the previously smooth upper bowl. Beds drift
  gently with bearing and fade continuously at both gallery mouths, reef and
  floor. One heightfield/material supplies geometry, normals and collision;
  no separate rock skins or hard-edged patches were added.
- Blue Hole horizon fade now spans 55–210 m (previously 90–380 m). Cooler,
  darker carbonate and weaker fine strata, staining and sediment contrast let
  the geometry supply the bed edges. Both sediment slots use silt.
- Low now resolves only the small Blue Hole focus with 16 subdivisions per
  survey cell, roughly 3.7 m spacing. The generic `resolveLow` profile opt-in
  is enabled only for this site. Low keeps its existing shader features,
  normal-map settings and scatter density. Other profiles retain their Low
  meshes. Titanic/Monterey site files and settings were not changed.
- Gallery builders and camera settings are unchanged. Ray tests preserve the
  High footprint measured against golden 132858 and the prior Low footprint,
  and check that terrain does not hide the pendant vertices in either layout.

Six local patches are refined. The Low census stays below 500k triangles even
with a 200k reserve for the submarine, life and effects. Across three upper-wall
bearings the rendered Low tread rises less than 3 m over 7 m, while its adjacent
riser rises more than 4 m over 4 m. Mesh/collision agreement is within 1 mm.
Noise-off analytic/mesh error at the old burial reproduction is now 0.73 m;
the regression now bounds it below 2 m instead of requiring the old >8 m error.
