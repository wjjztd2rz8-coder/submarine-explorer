## Decisions and research

1. **Lost City: clear fluid, no smoke or orange glow.** Existing mission overrides support `fluid: carbonate`, `smokeIntensity: 0`, `glowLights: 0`, and `shimmerStrength: 0.03`; carbonate props add no independent smoke. Both mission and free dive now use those settings. Journal text describes warm, clear alkaline flow. NOAA’s 2005 field description gives about 60,000 m², replacing the erroneous 500 m². Carbonate ages up to roughly 120,000 years refer to sampled field deposits, not the active Poseidon tower’s age. The biofilm objective now says scan rather than sample. Historical Beehive/IMAX markers remain distinct from a promised physical tower. [NOAA clear-fluid education sheet](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/okeanos/explorations/ex1104/background/edu/media/ex1104_tracking.pdf), [NOAA field extent](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/explorations/05lostcity/logs/july29/july29.html), [Ludwig et al. carbonate ages](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0016703711000135).
2. **Poseidon scale:** the model represents the composite edifice, not one narrow spire. Changed `dimensions_m` from `[70,70,60]` to `[100,100,60]`, preserving location, heading, and seabed snapping. Published height and width are about 60 and 100 m; the 100 m depth remains an authored footprint. Ground at the unchanged anchor is about 801 m in the offline tile. [Denny et al. 2016](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/59651).
3. **ASHES: proximity and mixed venting.** Moved Inferno’s prop and POI latitude from 45.93388 to 45.93379: about 10 m north of Mushroom rather than the previous 20 m, matching the published separation. Northward bearing is authored, not surveyed. Retained seabed snapping, dimensions, and heading. Mushroom has both clear-fluid outlets and small black-smoker chimlets, so the text no longer draws an absolute clear/smoke distinction. A 2,826 m² field occupies about one 43 × 61 m grid cell, not less than one. Removed stale eruption forecasts and corrected cabled-observatory attribution to OOI. [UW/OOI ASHES](https://interactiveoceans.washington.edu/research-sites/axial-caldera/ashes/).
4. **Blue Hole: retain the outer-atoll experience.** Catalogue and mission presentation now say Lighthouse Reef / Great Blue Hole and describe the existing outer slopes. The western grotto remains an authored limestone feature, not the measured hole interior. Research brief no longer promises an enclosed shaft or water-layer transition. The real hole’s roughly 320 m width, 124–125 m depth, and anoxic lower water remain sourced context; anoxia excludes reef fish, not all microbial life. No sinkhole terrain overlay exists within this package’s ownership. [Gischler et al. primary seismic/sediment study](https://limnogeology.ethz.ch/GischlerMarine.pdf).
5. **Bismarck: expose the depth gap, preserve support.** Published wreck depth remains approximately 4,790–4,791 m in catalogue/reference facts. The mission’s playable route now says 4,219 m and about 420 atmospheres, matching the shipped ground at the adopted expedition vicinity (offline 4,218.47 m). Journal and source notes explain the roughly 570 m discrepancy once. Removed unsupported claims that the coordinate is an upper impact position or that the slide explains the depth gap. The bow remains attached, the stern is missing, and main turrets are displaced; corrected the rudder hit to the evening before the final battle, turret separation, and survey chronology. Records differ on final sinking mechanisms, so attributed the 2001/2002 interpretations. The retained turret marker is an authored offset of about 900 m ENE. **Physical depth repair remains outstanding:** better bathymetry or an explicitly authored terrain reconstruction belongs to another package. No invented coordinates or underground hull offset introduced. [2001 expedition record: vicinity, depth, slide, wreck condition](https://www.hmshood.org.uk/hoodtoday/2001expedition/bismarck/encrypt.htm).
6. **Hunga: retain historical pre-January-2022 terrain.** The current shallow basin/route is consistently labelled historical. The 2026 GMRT fetch date does not identify the underlying survey vintage; historical staging is an inference from the shipped morphology, not a newly verified survey date. Primary repeat bathymetry reports the pre-eruption basin at about 150–200 m and the post-eruption deepest basin around 850 m. Corrected the 2014–15 cone/island connection, pressure to about 15–20 atmospheres for the historical basin, and removed unsupported casualty/unchanged-flank claims. The roughly 469 m objective is now a volcanic rim slope beyond the shallow basin, not an inner basin wall. [Ribó et al. 2026 repeat-bathymetry paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41561-026-02099-7).
7. **Hunga placement:** moved `caldera-tuff-wall` longitude from -175.418 to -175.420, about 208 m west, matching the existing scan marker at latitude -20.570. Preserved `[90,30,45]` dimensions, 270° heading, and seabed snapping. Offline ground is 468.59 m; compressed runtime terrain gives 471.14 m. Browser assertions verify shared XZ position and root contact with the runtime ground. Dimensions, heading, and wall profile remain scenic choices rather than measured inner-wall geometry.
8. **Beebe: retain the flank fragment.** Corrected the catalogue name to Beebe (Piccard); Von Damm is a separate shallower field, not an alternate name. Kept `[26,20,12]` prop dimensions instead of stretching a chimney to imitate the published approximately 80 m-wide, 50 m-high mound. Corrected supercritical-fluid text to measured low-salinity fluids around 401–403 °C at roughly 500 bar, rather than treating pure water’s critical point as sufficient proof at arbitrary salinity. Full mound foundations require independent geometry dimensions. [Webber et al. 2015](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/2015GC005879).
9. **Other accuracy and tone:** Challenger text attributes pool-depth findings to their surveys, dates the June 2020 measurement without calling it the latest, correctly places the deep outside the Monument boundary ([NOAA management plan](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/s3/2024-06/Mariana-Trench-Marine-National-Monument-Management-Plan-202405-FINAL.pdf)), aligns bottom-pressure text at roughly 1,100 bar, and no longer declares the Eastern Pool definitively deepest, claims no amphipod models, or uses the outdated 8,075 m fish record; the 2023 Izu–Ogasawara observations exceed 8,300 m. Blake explicitly calls healthy living cold-water coral naturally white. Titanic heritage date now matches the international agreement’s November 2019 entry into force. Endurance preservation no longer relies on an unsupported low-oxygen explanation; guide prose matches the preserved hull rather than the former block proxy. Its 350 gross register tons are correctly labelled tonnage, not displacement ([UK Antarctic Heritage Trust](https://ukaht.org/places/endurance-shipwreck/)). [2023 deepest-fish paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0967063723001711), [NOAA healthy white coral](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/news/million-mounds-news/), [UK Titanic agreement](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/agreement-concerning-the-shipwrecked-vessel-rms-titanic-ts-no82019).
10. **Player language:** replaced repeated darkness-below-a-few-hundred-metres claims with fading-sunlight guidance, retained concise wreck memorial/non-disturbance notes, and removed a game-wide collection prohibition that conflicted with the Phase F specimen loop. No new player prose uses “illustrative” or “reconstructed.” Source documents retain research/provenance explanations; they are not player caveats.
