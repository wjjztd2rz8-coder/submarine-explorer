## Confirmed facts and coordinate limits

- **Endurance:** CMP §2.1.4 confirms **68°44′21″S, 52°19′47″W**, **3,008 m**, and discovery **5 March 2022**. Sinking **21 November 1915**, launch **17 December 1912**, crew survival and rounded dimensions **44 × 7.6 m** agree with CMP §§2.1.1–2.1.4 (registered length 43.9 m). Endurance22 / FMHT discovery credit remains. The local hull scan depth **2,995 m** is above a **3,000.30 m grid**, not a replacement historical wreck depth. [E1–E2]
- **Endurance distance discrepancy:** the CMP and previous navigation audit support roughly five nautical miles from Worsley's logged fix; the press release's “four miles south” still differs. Preserve the existing attributed discussion, without inventing a reconciliation. [E1–E2]
- **Bismarck:** preserve **27 May 1941** sinking, **8 June 1989 / Ballard** discovery, **251 × 36 m** original ship dimensions, and published wreck depth about **4,790–4,791 m**. The expedition account gives only the **vicinity of 48°09′N, 16°07′W**, not an exact fix; the **4,218.47 m local grid** does not corroborate the wreck depth. The roughly **570 m discrepancy remains unresolved**. No terrain or wreck relocation was inferred. [B2–B3]
- **Bismarck unresolved details:** ~114 survivors versus sources reporting 115, exact complement, precise turret identities/coordinates and complete sinking mechanics remain source-dependent. Existing approximations and attribution are retained; no fabricated precision added. Model and debris dimensions remain tagged recreations.
- **Great Blue Hole:** **~320 m diameter, 125 m water depth**, anoxic lower water below **~90 m**, and central BH8 coring reference **17°18′57.2″N, 87°32′00.60″W** are directly supported by Gischler et al. 2025 §§1–2. That coring reference is not a surveyed gallery coordinate. UNESCO inscription in **1996** remains. [G1, G4]
- **Blue Hole historical date disagreement:** Fabien Cousteau's expedition account gives **1971**, while Gischler's introduction mentions **1970**. Retain 1971 with the explicit expedition source; the difference is unresolved. Neither is used to credit discovery. The **2018** sonar expedition is supported by the operator/participant accounts. [G1–G3]
- **Axial:** preserve **1998, 2011, 2015** eruption years, approximate summit **1,410 m**, caldera **3 × 8 km**, Mushroom **~4 m**, and Mushroom–Inferno separation **~10 m**. OOI's field reference is not proof of individual chimney offsets. [A1–A4]
- **Kama:** USGS reference **18.92°N, 155.27°W** is rounded; the existing catalogue coordinate **18.9167°N, 155.2667°W** rounds to it and was not shifted. Confirmed **~975 m summit**, **1955** former naming, **July 2021** renaming, **600 × 300 m** collapse pit. The Hiolo markers were selected by matching grid depth; that does not verify their geographic positions. Keep their existing recreation flags. [K1–K3]
