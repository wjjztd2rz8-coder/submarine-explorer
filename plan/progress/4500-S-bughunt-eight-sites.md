# 4500 — S-bughunt: Axial, Kamaʻehuakanaloa and Hunga spawn/scan geometry

**Supports V-eight-sites-lit.** Audited input: `56588911d3236f92540590e359619da374237b1b`, 2026-10-26. Read [DIRECTOR.md](../DIRECTOR.md) and [4360](4360-V-eight-sites-spawn-numerics.md) first. This package changes this note only: no production code, content tables, capability tests or screenshots; no push.

**Findings:** none of the three first objectives can be scanned from the near-site mission spawn, and all three are reachable with the real scanner and contact physics. All opening cameras and visible hulls clear the seabed. Axial’s Mushroom has clear line of sight but is tiny; Kama’s first Hiolo North chimney is completely hidden by its pillow-field prop; Hunga’s wall has clear line of sight and already exceeds 0.4% of the phone frame. Axial’s optional minor-chimney objective can be scanned without moving in Arcade.

This happens on all tiers, is not a first-primary spawn-scan bug, and was not changed.

For Kama, the proposed pillow centre moves ~44.5 m farther north and ~57.9 m west of its current position, behind the vent as seen from the opening. A size-only increase at its current location passed the area threshold but left the first chimney hidden; that candidate is rejected. Both proposals preserve the nominal scenic height and the real named chimneys’ dimensions; update the existing game-addition notes to describe the new invented footprint/location if a visual package adopts them. Expanding the footprints can affect later close exploration and needs Claude’s visual review and the capability suite before adoption. Scratch checks here establish framing, spawn/hull safety and scan range, not a complete pilot journey through the proposed scenery.

**Literal first-chimney limitation:** the proposals make the scenic subject at the first objective ≥0.4%; they do **not** make the individual Mushroom or Hiolo North chimney occupy that area. Mushroom remains ~0.009%, and the newly visible Hiolo North chimney ~0.002%. The existing mission table exposes range/bearing preference/yaw but no mission chase-radius override; lowering the mission range below the 100 m Arcade floor would enable a spawn scan. Enlarging a real 4 m chimney to satisfy this area target would contradict its factual size. A strict ≥0.4% requirement for the named chimney itself therefore needs a separate shared camera/framing design decision; do not present these scenic-context proposals as satisfying that stricter requirement. Hunga’s first subject itself already passes.
