## Findings and change

- Earlier Low portrait Lost City references contain pale carbonate and a lit
  seabed. Side growths are narrow leaning tubes with cut rims. The old code
  attaches to 75% of a smooth radius, which does not track the actual irregular
  wall or the lean of lesser columns.
- Fingers now raycast the parent column's actual local triangles, bury their
  broader feet, and inherit its transform. Stronger taper (top radius 25% → 8%
  of base), less lean (0.35–0.85 → 0.20–0.45 radians), lobed cross-sections,
  curved centreline and uneven crown replace the pipe profile. Existing vertex
  paint adds pale mineral crust; Low needs no normal-map detail to carry it.
- Earlier Titanic desktop reference shows the sub beside the bow contact;
  its earlier Low portrait has readable hull/sub and separate HUD panels.
  Monterey references show varied strata and readable life, with separated
  portrait telemetry, scan panel and controls. These are prior captures, not
  validation of this worktree. No Titanic/Monterey changes were made without
  fresh evidence of an S1/S2 regression.
- Site data, published facts, scan targets, parent columns, colliders, material
  programs and lighting settings are unchanged. All cuts/replacements are
  logged in `CHANGELOG.md`.
