# U-play-walkthrough: first-time player walkthrough

Date: 2026-11-01. Branch `claude/U-play-walkthrough`. No game code changed.

## What was captured

- Command: `PW_PORT=4871 PW_OUTDIR=dist-u-play npx playwright test tests/e2e/journal-debrief.spec.ts` (after `npm run build -- --outDir dist-u-play`). 62 passed.
- The spec's `progression` shots are a seeded Titanic mission dive only. They do not cover Home or site pick, so the review used the `journey` set from the same run: `.cache/codex/shots/journal-debrief/journey/round-1/<viewport>/<site>/01-home.png` through `13-return-home.png` (Home, pick site, briefing, dive start, 10 s in, Journal before any scan, debrief, Journal from debrief, assisted scan, return Home).
- Viewed in full: 1600x900 great-blue-hole, 390x844 titanic. 844x390 and 390x844 monterey-canyon exist but were not reviewed one by one.
- Gap: `03-briefing` and `04-briefing-actions` are identical, so no actions-state shot was captured.

## Findings (ranked)

1. **U-debrief-surface-copy [Codex]:** the debrief says "Back at the surface" while the sub is submerged. Shots: `journey/round-1/1600x900/great-blue-hole/09-debrief-unscanned.png` (41 m), `journey/round-1/390x844/titanic/09-debrief-unscanned.png` (3765 m), `journey/round-1/390x844/titanic/12-debrief-assisted-scan.png` (3781 m, SPEED 0.0 kn). Target: headline and "surfaced" copy only when depth is under about 10 m; mid-dive check-ins use a neutral title such as "Check-in" or "Paused". Add a test that asserts the headline against depth.
2. **U-touch-tutorial-copy [Haiku]:** on a touch phone the first-step banner says "Left stick: follow the marker." Shot: `journey/round-1/390x844/titanic/05-dive-start.png`. Target: touch wording such as "Drag the stick to follow the marker." Keep controller wording only when a controller is connected.
3. **U-home-two-primaries [Sonnet]:** after the first dive the home panel shows "Dive: <site>" as the teal primary button and a "Daily dive" card with its own ☆☆☆ directly below. Shots: `journey/round-1/1600x900/great-blue-hole/13-return-home.png`, `journey/round-1/390x844/titanic/13-return-home.png`. Target: one primary action on Home. Put the daily dive under Dive sites, or show it only on the first visit.
4. **U-dive-hud-stack [Sonnet]:** dive start on desktop stacks four blocks at once: sonar, the step banner, the mission panel and the depth/heading/speed panel. The step banner and the mission panel repeat the same goal, and the mission panel's first line ("Head to the stalactite gallery") differs from the briefing's ("Scan the stalactite gallery"). Shots: `journey/round-1/1600x900/great-blue-hole/05-dive-start.png`, `06-dive-10s.png`. Target: two HUD blocks in the first 60 s (step banner plus compact depth). Show the mission panel after the first objective, and use one name per objective everywhere.
5. **U-site-list-noise [Sonnet]:** the pick-site list gives every row ☆☆☆ and "0/N logged" before the player has done anything, and the globe pins have no labels (a bare "2" cluster badge). On phone the globe is a sliver behind the sheet. Shots: `journey/round-1/1600x900/great-blue-hole/02-pick-site.png`, `journey/round-1/390x844/titanic/02-pick-site.png`. Target: show stars and logged counts only for sites visited; highlight one "Recommended first dive" row; label the first pins or drop the cluster badge.
6. **U-briefing-jargon [Sonnet]:** the briefing uses "Class A - coastal (rated 1,000 m)" and "Class B - deep ocean (rated 6,500 m)" as HULL, and the PRIMARY/OPTIONAL tags, the mode caption and the Advanced toggle are all very small type. Shots: `journey/round-1/1600x900/great-blue-hole/03-briefing.png`, `journey/round-1/390x844/titanic/03-briefing.png`. Target: plain-language hull line ("Rated to 1,000 m"), tags at least 11 px, and the Advanced control moved into a closed disclosure.
7. **U-journal-first-visit [Sonnet]:** the first Journal visit shows a three-level nav with 0/25 species, 0/8 animals, 0/3 secrets, 55 logged and a spoilers toggle before anything has been found. On phone the site page stacks two prose blocks above the first game-relevant line. Shots: `journey/round-1/1600x900/great-blue-hole/07-journal-unscanned.png`, `journey/round-1/390x844/titanic/07-journal-unscanned.png`. Target: first visit opens Front page with one "Next: scan the stalactite gallery" line; hide zero-count categories until the first find; trim the site page to one blurb.
8. **U-sonar-labels [Sonnet]:** the sonar's "120 m", "80 m" and "1000 m" labels overlap, and the "0 m" label sits under the own-ship marker. Shots: `journey/round-1/1600x900/great-blue-hole/03-briefing.png`, `05-dive-start.png`. Target: keep the range label only at default zoom, and move or drop the own-ship's "0 m".

Honest notes: the debrief copy and the Home card were the most likely to confuse a first-time player. Phone dive start and the briefing are otherwise clean and readable, and the Titanic opening framing is good.
