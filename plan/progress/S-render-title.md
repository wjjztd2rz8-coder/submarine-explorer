## Deleted duplication and preserved behavior

TitleScene now owns one Headlights instance and recreates a MarineSnow instance when its snow tier changes. It no longer implements spotlight construction, beam geometry/material/shaders, beam quaternion/reach calculation, seeded snow buffers, per-particle wrapping/drift, or geometry/material teardown loops. Its parallel lamp/beam/material/base-buffer arrays and generic texture tracking list are deleted. Both title beams share one geometry and material instead of allocating two identical pairs.

The remaining title code supplies authored data and composition: lamp offsets, seabed pool target sampling, sprite texture, count per tier, motion time and field centre. Camera/framing, terrain/contact shadow and vehicle ownership stay in TitleScene. No UI, settings, player capability, guidance surface, site branch, global-config mutation or preset substitution is introduced. The shared inputs are plain constructor data and a world-target/absolute-time placement method; no new rendering framework or policy registry is added. Supporting both existing shading models adds typed inputs to the helpers, while removing duplicate ownership and construction from TitleScene; this is a responsibility/allocation simplification, not a claim of fewer aggregate source lines.
