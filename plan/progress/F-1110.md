# F-1110 — five-site factual copy audit

Date: 2026-10-09. Scope: Lost City, Beebe/Piccard, Great Blue Hole,
Monterey Canyon and Titanic. Two passes: primary-source comparison, then
cross-check of the shipped catalogue, scan/mission copy and Journal provenance.

## Plan and coverage

1. Trace `src/game/Guide.ts`, `Pois.ts`, `JournalData.ts`, and
   `src/ui/Journal.ts`, `FieldGuide.ts`, `ScanOverlay.ts` to their shipped data.
   Review catalogue summaries/facts, guide paragraphs/facts/sources, POI names
   and placement notes, mission briefing/objective copy, secrets and linked life
   entries for all five sites.
2. Compare site claims with primary expedition accounts, original research,
   university reports and data-owner documentation. Separate publication dates
   from survey dates, and published site measurements from game marker values.
3. Correct contradictions, trim unsupported precision, retain the existing
   single Journal provenance tag, document cuts, and run the repository gates.

No source runtime change is needed: `journalEntryTag()` already emits exactly
one provenance value. `Journal.renderEntry()` renders it once in the title row,
and the Journal introduction explains it once. Guide or POI
`reconstruction: true` produces **Recreation**; each secret and staged wildlife
entry gets **Game addition**. Terrain-only observations retain their source
status. Entries remain linked to the original scan IDs and unlocks.

## Changes and cuts

- `data/landmarks.json`: Titanic's summary credits the joint French–American
  leadership. Replace the five sites' player-facing Wikipedia links with primary
  references. Coordinate-source metadata remains a record of the original pin
  provenance; it is not upgraded to a verified survey coordinate.
- `data/landmarks/lost-city/pois.json`: replace secondary citations, correct the
  Beehive-marker distance, and remove the claim that the microbial marker is a
  verified sampling location attributable to a single 2003 cruise. The retained
  OBIS records have no collection depths; the exact footprint is unverified.
- `data/landmarks/beebe-vent-field/{guide,pois,mission}.json`: correct survey
  year/name, mark both authored smoker/mound positions for Recreation tagging,
  lower the western mound's placement confidence, and correct shrimp distance.
- `data/landmarks/great-blue-hole/{guide,pois}.json`: researcher/operator
  citations for the sinkhole and gallery, scientific context for the reef
  platform, no universal 40–50 m stalactite-depth assertion.
- `data/landmarks/monterey-canyon/pois.json`: remove the unsupported shelf-break
  identification and cite MBARI/GMRT for the grid-based upper-channel marker.
- `data/landmarks/titanic/{guide,pois,mission}.json`: remove the unverified 600–800 m
  dense-field lengths, 5 km² dense-field area and southwest-trail interpretation;
  retain the independently supported broad survey area. Remove the precise
  boiler count/room assignment at the marker and inferred breakup point, bow/
  stern remnant lengths, 18 m burial estimate, unverified bow-capstan plaque,
  1996 disappearance date and exact aft-starboard memorial footprint. Remove
  the unsupported local channel-forming-current attribution. Trim the casualty
  range, distance offshore and exact discovery time to supported site facts.
- Titanic's Big Piece entry uses the recovering operator's **17-ton** description
  with the unit system explicitly unspecified; remove 15–20 **short** tons,
  unsourced dimensions and current exhibit-location claims. Keep its 1998
  recovery and absence from the seabed, without treating the marker as its
  surveyed historical footprint.
- Titanic's protection copy now uses NOAA's concrete US–UK agreement date and
  in situ policy, replacing the broad claim of automatic UNESCO protection.
- `data/life/life.json`: align Beebe shrimp depth and nutrition with the guide.
  Secrets and other staged animal entries retain their existing Game addition
  tag; no new claim of a local biological survey is made.

All cuts are also recorded under Changed/Removed in `CHANGELOG.md`. Historical
site `sources.md` files link to this review so obsolete secondary-only notes do
not silently reinstate the removed claims. The memorial objective hint no longer asserts the plaque is absent.
No POI, objective or Journal entry
is removed; original IDs, coordinates, scan timing and geometry remain intact.

## Access limits

Several older URLs failed direct retrieval: NOAA's 2005 Lost City log, the ETH
2013 Blue Hole PDF, Connelly's Nature landing page and the PNAS 2009-plume paper.
The discovery-survey mound dimensions (about 80 m diameter and 50 m high)
are also present in [Nye’s Southampton thesis](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/362004/1/Nye_2C_20Verity_PhD.pdf).
The separation from Von Damm (~21 km) is corroborated by the
[researchers’ NERC abstract](https://nora.nerc.ac.uk/id/eprint/510856/).
Existing facts already corrected in F-FACTCHECK-630 were retained where the fresh
primary accounts agree; the 60,000 m² field estimate was reconfirmed in the
[indexed primary NOAA log](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/explorations/05lostcity/logs/july29/july29.html),
with the direct-fetch limitation retained. Paull's publisher page
failed, but its full paper was read through the authors' NERC repository. PMC
sometimes served a captcha; the opsin abstract was available in indexed PMC and
the authors' Okayama repository. Streit’s publisher abstract was indexed even
when a direct page open failed. Wikipedia/news results were not verification
evidence for new or replacement claims.

was restored. Journal screenshots are retained in `.cache/codex/shots-f1110/`;
desktop/phone screenshots are in `test-results-f1110-final/`, and smoke captures
