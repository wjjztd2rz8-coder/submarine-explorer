Falls short: the slope still reads brownish-olive under the lamp rather than neutral grey; the tower is only moderately bright (not luminous); foot rubble is present but sparse and mostly hidden behind the sub in the opening pose; Low shows a very smooth featureless slope with slightly coarse silhouette.
