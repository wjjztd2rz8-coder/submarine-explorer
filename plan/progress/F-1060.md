# F-1060 — Journal groups and compact debrief

2026-10-09. Implementation complete; browser acceptance and screenshots remain
pending because this sandbox cannot start a preview or Chromium and has no
connected browser.

## Changes

- Journal site rows expand/collapse their entries with `aria-expanded` and
  `aria-controls`. The current site opens by default; other sites stay collapsed
  until opened. Opening another site also shows its overview. Closing a site
  leaves its article on screen.
- Native category disclosures group About the site, Points of interest, Species,
  Wildlife and Secrets. Points of interest opens initially; the other categories
  start collapsed. Counts stay in the group headers; unscanned targets retain
  the single “more to find” row. No target names leak with spoilers off.
- Group choices persist through discovery/spoiler refreshes and site toggles.
  Direct entry navigation and focused scans reveal the relevant site/category.
  Rebuilt controls restore keyboard focus using FocusTrap's existing visibility
  rules. Opening a modal still blurs its opener without focusing a control;
  closing it restores the opener. No HUD elements were added.
- Site rows and category summaries have 44 px touch targets. Categories use
  native Enter/Space behavior; collapsed contents are excluded from the trap.
  Phone Contents continues to switch between the list and article.
- Entries retain exactly one provenance tag: Game addition for wildlife/secrets,
  Recreation for recreated POIs. Entry copy, sources and unlocks are unchanged.
- Debriefs show a compact score row (stars/research points, scans and objectives),
  one highlight and one next suggestion. Distance/depth/time cells and long
  discovery/Journal/exploration lists were removed from the card. Abort causes
  remain visible as the highlight with a recovery suggestion. Partial dives
  remain warm. One primary action and the existing Journal/resume/More actions
  remain available.
