# Owner phone test, 2026-10-18 (d0a0a69, iPhone 17 Pro, Safari)

> Decision record. Status and order of these packages live in [DIRECTOR.md](DIRECTOR.md) as the `P2-*` items.

The owner's notes, condensed, with Claude's decision for each one. Device: iPhone 17 Pro, so a weaker phone will do worse than this.

## What was checked in code before deciding

- **Boost works but can't be seen.** In Arcade it raises top speed from about 12 to 20.6 m/s. But the touch toggle only adds a faint glow, there is no meter, and `progress.ts` turns the latch off silently once the 12.8 s budget runs out.
- **Scan range is set per point of interest.** Titanic's bow and stern use `radius_m: 30` (36 m once the 1.2 scan bonus applies), which is why scanning needed a very close approach. Other scan targets range from 30 to 300 m.
- **Events are random.** They only occur at six sites (Axial, Hunga, Beebe, Hudson, Monterey, Kamaʻehu), fire 70-160 s into active dive time, and need the sub low over the seabed (shallow water for whales). There is nothing for the player to do or find, and the owner's dives were mostly at sites with no events.
- **Auto tier:** `detectTier` (`src/core/Quality.ts`) reads the WebGL renderer string, core count, memory (hidden by Safari) and screen size. Phones get Medium only when the GPU is a recent flagship and they report at least 8 cores; every other phone gets Low. Dynamic resolution then lowers the pixel ratio when frames run long. Settings shows "Auto" without saying which tier it chose.
- **UI scale already exists** (Settings → Accessibility → UI scale). It is just hard to find.
- **The Lost City sub** is the Class A (coastal) hull, which Lost City and Blue Hole use. Titanic and Beebe use other hulls, which is why the problem only showed there.
