# Owner phone test, 2026-10-11 (f51, about 5 minutes)

The owner's notes, condensed, with Claude's decision for each one. Where a phone fix also helps on desktop, it ships on both. Fresh captures of the current build: `.cache/phonefb/phone-titanic/` and `.cache/phonefb/phoneland-titanic/`.

| #   | Owner note                                                                                                                             | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Package                                                |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Globe pins are fanned out in rings, so you can't tell what they point at. Put dots on their real locations, with bigger touch targets. | Drop the ring fan-out and its leader lines. Each pin sits on its true location: a small visible dot with a hit area of at least 44 px. Pins that overlap merge into one cluster pin showing a count, and tapping it zooms in until they separate. Card-only catalogue sites become faint specks, not interactive by default. The globe gets more room in phone portrait.           | f-globe-pins (Sonnet)                                  |
| 2   | Too much text at every menu. Facts and hazards belong in "learn more", consolidated and de-duplicated, on the web version too.         | The briefing shows the objectives, one summary line, the memorial note and the dive settings. Facts and hazards move into a single collapsed "About this site" section with no duplicates; the hull-rating hazard repeats the meta line, so it goes. The same goes for the site page in the Journal.                                                                               | f-text-diet (Sonnet); Journal side in f-journal-mobile |
| 3   | "View controls" lists keys, which is useless on touch. A help or tutorial page might help on mobile.                                   | Remove the Controls section and "View controls" from the briefing. On touch, the pause menu's "Controls" becomes "How to play": a picture-first page covering the stick, slider, buttons, drag to look, pinch and double tap. Desktop keeps its key list.                                                                                                                          | f-text-diet                                            |
| 4   | Titanic spawns right in front of the objective, which kills exploration. Give the player a direction to navigate first.                | Arcade missions open on an approach: the first primary target sits 150 to 300 m ahead, out of scan range, with the waypoint and sonar pointing at it, and the first objective reads as "head to …". Free dives keep their composed beauty openings. This replaces the 2026-10 rule "missions open within 300 m of a primary, framed".                                              | f-first-dive-flow (Codex)                              |
| 5   | Scanning takes several seconds, but the tutorial moves on as soon as it starts.                                                        | The scan step completes on `scan:complete` only, and the card shows "Scanning…" while the scan is held.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | f-first-dive-flow                                      |
| 6   | Control notes like "press Space to take a picture" are no help on the phone.                                                           | Remove every key name from touch text: the photo-mode capture label and tips, contextual hints (touch currently says "E" for the ROV), and "OR PRESS ENTER".                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | f-text-diet; hint labels in f-first-dive-flow          |
| 7   | Why is the Journal hidden in the pause menu?                                                                                           | Add a Journal button to the dive HUD: next to Pause on touch, and a small button on desktop too (J still works).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | f-touch-controls (Codex)                               |
| 8   | Boost should be a toggle on mobile, at the far left with the rise and sink controls.                                                   | Touch boost becomes a toggle (tap on, tap off; it also switches off when the boost meter empties or the dive is paused). It moves to the far left, docked above the stick, away from the action cluster. Note: the rise/sink slider is on the right, so "far left" and "with rise/sink" conflict; this follows "far left" and can move. Desktop keeps hold-to-boost.               | f-touch-controls                                       |
| 9   | The Journal is hard to navigate on mobile.                                                                                             | Phone layout: a compact sticky header (title, back, close), list-first navigation (sites, then a site page, then an entry) with large rows, and spoilers moved out of the header. Desktop gets the same de-duplication.                                                                                                                                                            | f-journal-mobile (Sonnet, after the Claude reset)      |
| 10  | The "Data: GMRT" note on the sonar doesn't matter during play.                                                                         | Remove it from the dive HUD. The full CC BY credit moves to a "Data credits" entry in the pause menu and to the site page in the Journal, one tap away, so the licence is still met.                                                                                                                                                                                               | f-text-diet                                            |
| 11  | Events like the snow one are odd: no explanation, and visually nothing.                                                                | Cut the marine-snow event, since snow is already ambient everywhere. Reform the plume, turbidity and whale events: each starts in view ahead of the sub, gets a stronger visual, and a caption that says what it is and why in one line; witnessing one logs it in the debrief and Journal. Any event still unreadable on the Low tier gets cut. Every cut is logged in CHANGELOG. | f-events-reform (Codex)                                |
| 12  | Performance is great.                                                                                                                  | Keep it: no new per-frame cost; the event visuals stay a single draw.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | all                                                    |
